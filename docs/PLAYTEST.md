## Same script for every player

1. Hand over the device and say, "Start a run and keep playing until it ends." Do not explain the controls.
2. Watch the first touch, crowd changes, lane choices, and boss warning. Do not coach. If help is requested, give the smallest prompt and record it.
3. After the run, say, "Open the Shop and equip **____________________**." Use the named owned item above.
4. When the shop task ends, stay quiet for 15 seconds. Record whether the player asks for or starts another run without invitation.

Codes: **Y** observed without help, **P** observed after a prompt, **N** not observed, **NA** not reached. "Crowd" means their words or choices show that they noticed the crowd grow or shrink. "Lane" means at least one intentional lane choice. "Boss" means they react to or mention the warning.

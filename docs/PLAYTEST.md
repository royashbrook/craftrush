## Same script for every player

1. Hand over the device and say, "Start a run and keep playing until it ends." Do not explain the controls.
2. Watch the first touch, firing, crowd changes, lane choices, and boss warning. Do not coach. If help is requested, give the smallest prompt and record it.
3. After the run, say, "Open the Shop and equip **____________________**." Use the named owned item above.
4. When the shop task ends, stay quiet for 15 seconds. Record whether the player asks for or starts another run without invitation.

Codes: **Y** observed without help, **P** observed after a prompt, **N** not observed, **NA** not reached. "Crowd" means their words or choices show that they noticed the crowd grow or shrink. "Lane" means at least one intentional lane choice. "Boss" means they react to or mention the warning.

## v1.9 core-loop focus

Run Bow Blitz first on NORMAL. A successful causality test means the player
learns, without explanation, that holding fires, releasing stops, dragging aims,
and shooting can improve a gate. Ask after the run, "What did your finger do?"
and "Why did your crowd get bigger or smaller?" Record their words, not an
interpretation. On the boss, watch for a visible reaction to both armor breaks.

The release signal is behavioral: at least 4 of 5 fire within three seconds, at
least 4 of 5 can explain one input-to-outcome link, and at least 3 of 5 start or
ask for another run without invitation. A win rate is not a fun signal by itself.
